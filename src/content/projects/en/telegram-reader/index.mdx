This is a project I had been sitting on for several years, and only now finally got around to finishing. Meet **Telegram Reader**.

The service lets you read public Telegram channels, subscribe to them,
and maintain access even if Telegram is blocked — provided that Cloudflare itself remains accessible.

When you first subscribe, an anonymous account is created to store all your channels.
You can save the `Account ID` in the settings to sync your subscriptions across devices or to avoid losing access.

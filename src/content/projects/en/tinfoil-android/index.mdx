My second venture into the Nintendo Switch world. Just like the first one, the app is straightforward and focuses
on a single task — running a Tinfoil server right on your Android device.

If you don't have a computer nearby but still want to host your own game library on the go, this app is for you.
All you need to do is select the directory containing your games, tap "Start server", and follow the instructions in the "Tinfoil setup on Switch" section.

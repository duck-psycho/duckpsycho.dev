A small app for short-term memory training with built-in progress tracking. 
The concept is simple: instead of just memorizing numbers, you have to mentally reverse the digit order and enter the answer.

I originally built it for myself while learning Android development, but eventually, I wanted to go through the entire journey:
from registering a Google Play Console account to testing and final publishing.

I'll probably write a post later about publishing my first app on Google Play — covering privacy policy generation,store page setup, and most importantly,
the 14-day testing phase with 20 testers (yes, those are the current Google Play requirements, though they don't apply to everyone).
